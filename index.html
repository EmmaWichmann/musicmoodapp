MusicMoodApp

How do you feel now — and how do you want to feel when the music ends?

Most music apps start by asking what you want to hear. This one starts with what you need: your mood, your energy, how much time you have, and where you're trying to go emotionally. Build a listening journey, check in with a Context Mode for the moment you're in, and see the patterns in how music actually affects you over time.

What's the situation, not just the mood?

Choose what you're actually doing. The app suggests a fitting energy range and time budget instead of asking you to translate "studying" into a genre yourself.

Emotional Journey Builder

From how you feel, to how you want to feel

Not a static playlist — a structured path. Pick a starting feeling, a destination feeling, and how much time you have, and the app builds a small sequence of stages that move gradually between them.

Did it actually help?

A short check-in after listening — not a diagnosis. Over time this builds a picture of what tends to help you, in your own words.
